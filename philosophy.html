atom plenum

philosophy

Put in a spatial context, the atomic-plenum debate is also one between the Newtonian view of space as inert container populated with objects, and the more contemporary, dynamic view whereby space is a "plenum" characterized by a ubiquitous field. As Einstein (1960) wrote in Relativity, There is no such thing as empty space, i.e. space without field. Space-time does not claim existence on its own, but only as a structural quality of the field.
